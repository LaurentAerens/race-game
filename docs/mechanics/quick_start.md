# Quick Start

You are the team principal of **Horizon Racing**, starting in the **National Open Cup (Tier 3)**. Here is what you need to know to get through your first season.

---

## Your Starting Situation

- **Series:** National Open Cup — Tier 3 (sprint-only weekends, no main race)
- **Starting cash:** Varies by difficulty — from +$5M (Very Easy) to -$4M (Very Hard) vs the Normal baseline
- **Season length:** 18 weeks

### Tier 3 Limits

In Tier 3, many controls are locked until you promote:

| System | Tier 3 |
|--------|--------|
| Pace modes | NORMAL and PUSH only |
| Engine modes | STANDARD only |
| ERS | Not available (`N/A`) |
| Custom R&D | BRAKES and FRONT_WING only |

---

## Race Weekend Flow

Each race weekend runs: **FP1 → FP2 → Qualifying → Sprint**

1. **Free Practice (5 laps each):** Choose a session plan — `FAST_LAP`, `LONG_RUNS`, `SPRINT_STINTS`, or `BALANCED`. These build setup confidence and feed your virtual car simulation.
2. **Qualifying (3 laps):** Sets your starting grid position.
3. **Sprint:** Runs for `max(7, round(total_circuit_laps × 0.38))` laps. Points awarded to top 8 finishers: 8-7-6-5-4-3-2-1.

---

## During the Race

Use the **Driver Strategy Panel** at the bottom of the screen to:

- Switch pace between **NORMAL** and **PUSH** (CONSERVE and ATTACK unlock in T2)
- Call a **pit stop** via the **BOX** button (opens the Pit Strategy Modal)
- Monitor **fuel (kg)**, **tyre wear (%)**, and **component health**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> There is **no fuel refueling** during pit stops. Fuel starts at 50 kg and only depletes. Running PUSH pace increases tyre and component wear — balance performance with longevity.

Watch the **component health bars** (FW / RW / BRK / ENG). Any component reaching **0% = instant retirement**.

---

## Early Season Priorities

1. **Control cash flow** — Monthly expenses include: facility upkeep, staff wages, and driver salaries. Check the budget screen weekly.
2. **Invest in R&D** — In T3 you can develop BRAKES and FRONT_WING. Allocate engineering budget to next-gen chassis as well.
3. **Staff your facilities** — Empty Department Head or Director slots hurt output significantly. Fill vacancies early.
4. **Sign sponsors** — Base T3 monthly retainer is $260k. Pay-drivers bring extra sponsor income at the cost of weaker race performance.
5. **Develop drivers** — Young drivers (under 28) grow each race. Race position matters: P1 gives a 3.0× development multiplier vs 0.55× at P17+.

---

## The Goal: Promotion

Win the **National Open Cup championship** to earn promotion to Tier 2. You may decline promotion if you won it. Promotion resets car components to T2 baseline specs and roughly **doubles your sponsor income** (~1.87× per-race).
